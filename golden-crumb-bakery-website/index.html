OUR STORY

A little bakery with a lot of heart.

Golden Crumb began with one sourdough starter, a tiny oven and a simple idea: everyday food should still feel special.

We bake in small batches using traditional techniques, locally sourced ingredients where possible, and absolutely no shortcuts on the good stuff.
